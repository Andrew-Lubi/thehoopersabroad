Update 1

We’re here!

22 November 2026 · Surop

A long one, covering 10 days. Future instalments will be shorter!!

The long journey

After a very frantic final few days getting our house ready to be left for 18 months, we finally left home at 3pm on Thursday 12 November full of anticipation, hoping for a smooth journey but certainly not expecting one having had flight problems in our two previous trips.

We did have a couple of minor incidents at Heathrow. The first happened just as we were about to go through security when Dad went to get passports and boarding passes ready to show security. But where were they? Nowhere to be found! Not in the backpack pocket where they live, not put down next to the bins where Daddy had to empty the water bottles, not with Mummy. Aaaaagghh! We’ve lost everything!!

Oh, hang on a minute - here they are in Daddy’s back pocket!! 😂

The second happened as we went through security: bags in trays, belts off and through the x-ray machine. But a bag has gone off to be inspected! We’re sure we haven’t got anything we’re not allowed so what can be wrong?! The security guard starts to take things out. It’s not the tablet, no problem there, or the water bottle. Oh - but what’s this? It’s Bone Head!! That’s the problem! Bone Head has the exact same density as an explosive so he set off the alarm!!! Luckily, Bone Head is only a book so we were allowed to have him back. Phew!!

We took off from Heathrow at 7.30pm and after a stop in Abu Dhabi, we arrived in Manila at 2pm on Friday 13th (10pm local time). From there we had a 7.5 hour wait before a 2 hour internal flight to Davao City on the island of Mindanao so we had time to snap a photo of the colourful Jeepney because they’re a Filipino tradition.

We got to our hotel at around 1am on Saturday 14th (9am local time). That's a 34 hour journey!! Andrei and Alexander coped amazingly but you can see from the photo of the boys at Davao airport, they definitely look less fresh than when we left!!

Davao City - a few days of jobs

After spending Saturday doing very little, we then had a few days to get as many jobs done as possible. Both Mum and Dad had dentists and opticians to see and there was loads of shopping to do. All the big shopping has to be done in Davao because down at our home in Surop, there is very little. So books and toys were a priority (we have a birthday coming up) and all sorts of things we knew we’d be needing for the house. The house is well set up for a short holiday but not a long stay so there is quite a lot to do.

While those were the adult priorities, the boys of course had priorities of their own. First up - bug hunting and it wasn’t long before Andrei was carrying a cricket around on his hand. However, that was nothing compared with the nest of huge millipedes they found! Thank goodness they don’t bite!!

Our resort also had a zipwire and a pool with a great water slide which the boys absolutely loved.

We also had two cousins, Antonette and Kristel around to help entertain the boys while Mum and Dad got on with the boring stuff.

Travelling to Surop

Surop is our village in the municipality of Governor Generoso, in the province of Davao Oriental.

On our way we saw lots of scooters and tricycles which are the way most people still travel. The tricycles are made of a motorbike with a metal frame attached which has a seat (or luggage space) and the whole thing has a box round it to help keep off the worst of the rain. It does rain very often here which is why the landscape is so green but it usually doesn’t last long and it’s so warm it’s like having a little shower!

I enjoyed the lorry with its “More Prayer, More Power” sign on the back. People here very often have statements of Faith on their vehicles, including on their boats and I’m going to try and make a collection of photos because some of them are lovely.

Finally home

Here’s a glimpse of where we live: our house, our street (the photo at the top of this post, taken on a previous trip), the coconut tree in our garden, our first harvest and our first jug of coconut water which is just THE BEST drink ever!!!

Our church celebrated Thanksgiving yesterday. It’s literally opposite our house and our Pastor is Ana’s uncle. The boys call him Papou and he’s a wonderful friend to us when we’re here. You can see the church is beautifully decorated and the service was followed by a wonderful feast.

And finally (for now); what’s the first thing we do when we get to Surop? We get a haircut!!

That’s it for instalment 1. I’ll try to do these at least fortnightly and I’ll try to find a way to alert you when we post it.

Bye for now!!
